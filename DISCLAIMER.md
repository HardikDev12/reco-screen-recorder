# General Software & Media Recording Disclaimer

The RECO screen recording software is provided "as is" and "as available".

---

### 1. Hardware & System Compatibility
While RECO is engineered to support auto-detection for NVIDIA NVENC, AMD AMF, Intel QSV, and x264 software encoding, performance and recording frame rates vary depending on individual hardware configurations, GPU drivers, display resolutions, and operating system load.

---

### 2. Audio & Video Integrity
The developers of RECO do not guarantee that recordings will be uninterrupted, continuous, or completely free of dropped frames or audio sync drift under severe system strain or hardware crashes. Users are strongly encouraged to perform brief test recordings prior to capturing critical non-repeatable events.

---

### 3. Data Loss & Storage
RECO writes recordings directly to user-designated local storage directories. The developers are not liable for lost, corrupted, overwritten, or inaccessible files resulting from insufficient disk space, improper process termination, third-party antivirus interference, or storage drive failure.

---

### 4. Legal & Regulatory Compliance
RECO does not assess, monitor, or verify whether any specific screen or audio recording conforms with local consent laws, workplace regulations, intellectual property protections, or confidentiality covenants.
