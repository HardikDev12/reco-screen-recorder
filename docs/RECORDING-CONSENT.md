# Recording Consent & Compliance Notice

## Important Notice on Screen and Audio Recording Laws

Laws governing the recording of audio, video, telephone calls, virtual meetings, and screen content vary significantly across countries, states, and regional jurisdictions.

---

### Key Principles

1. **User Responsibility**: The user of RECO is solely responsible for determining the legal requirements that apply to their specific recording activities.
2. **Two-Party / All-Party Consent Jurisdictions**: In many jurisdictions (such as several US states and international regions), it is illegal to record an audio conversation or video meeting without the explicit consent of **all participants**.
3. **Workplace and Confidentiality Policies**: Recording proprietary business software, customer data, healthcare records, or non-public meetings may violate employment agreements, non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), or privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA).
4. **Copyright & Intellectual Property**: Recording copyrighted media, streaming broadcasts, or commercial training courses for redistribution without permission may infringe upon intellectual property rights.

---

### Software Role
RECO is a general-purpose recording utility. The software does not monitor, evaluate, or restrict the content you record, nor does it provide legal advice. Always ensure you have obtained all necessary permissions and consents prior to initiating a recording.
